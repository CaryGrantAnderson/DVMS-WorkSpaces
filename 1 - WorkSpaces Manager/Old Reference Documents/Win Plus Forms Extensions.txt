Win Plus Forms Extensions
=========================
1/30/2013


   - Message Bar (Top or Bottom or both).
	
	- Replaces Status Bar.

	- Can grow and compress in height.

   - No menu "bars" (as in DevExpress).

	- Menus compress to a single universal Icon
	  which can be expanded moved and docked 
	  and displayed in different forms including
	  toolbar, ribbon, Button Bar, TREE!

		- And depending upon type at the 
		  same time!

		- Users can create their own Menu
		  Views!
