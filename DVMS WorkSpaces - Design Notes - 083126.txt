DVMS WorkSpaces - A Super Fusion Personal Dashboard/Desktop/Organizer

The WorkSpaces idea is to have an app which strives to be the fusion of a personal desktop dashboard and work organizer and advances the Windows Desktop paradigm to a more advanced app level that fuses and integrates multiple applications and tools for a more advanced desktop environment and experience.  WorkSpaces is dynamic and consists of a gridded work space where the user can add, edit, and mix and match a variety of Buttons, Widgets, and Mini Apps with integration into regular full apps and tools.  It will provide a number of centralized common functions including work logging and tracking, alerts and notifications, app IPC, and WIWA (Where I Was At) functionality.  In use, it presents a digital representation of multiple workspaces to the user when the user can more efficiently see status, access and use apps and tools, and strives towards a Data and App Fusion Convergence.
